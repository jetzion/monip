## Details

MONIP Group brings together global sourcing expertise, creative product development, and strategic merchandising to help cruise lines and retail businesses stand out in competitive markets. We go beyond finding products. We identify opportunities, develop tailored merchandise programs, and connect ideas with the right products, suppliers, and retail strategies. From concept and design to sourcing and market execution, we deliver solutions shaped around our clients' brands, customers, and business objectives.

With an international network and a diverse portfolio spanning distinctive souvenirs, collectibles, gift sets, specialty merchandise, and sustainable products, we transform market insights into compelling retail offerings. Our approach combines commercial thinking, attention to detail, and a commitment to quality and responsible sourcing. By building strong partnerships and understanding what drives purchasing decisions, MONIP Group helps clients create memorable shopping experiences, strengthen customer engagement, and unlock new opportunities for growth.
